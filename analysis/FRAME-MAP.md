# FRAME-MAP — Temporal map of the 327 frames

Scene boundaries were found by inter-frame luminance difference (mean |Δ| on a 320 × 178 downsample)
and confirmed against the contact sheets in `analysis/atlas/`.

## Cross-cutting observations

* **The header never hides or shrinks** in any of the 327 frames.
* **The native cursor is never hidden**; the ring follower exists only over the hero canvas.
* **No accent colour appears in the UI layer** at any point — only inside the WebGL scenes.
* **Every major heading uses the same per-glyph blur-resolve** (Scenes 03, 08, 09, 15, 19, 22, 25, 26).
* **Two scrubbed pinned scenes** (Scenes 11–14 and 16–18) account for ~80 of the 327 frames — roughly
  a quarter of the entire experience.
* **Capture artifacts to ignore:** rounded window frame, right scrollbar, the `1.0x` recorder pill,
  the bottom-left URL tooltip, the screen-share bar in 325–327, and the tearing bands in
  076–079 / 178–181 / 243–244 / 284–285 / 320–322.
